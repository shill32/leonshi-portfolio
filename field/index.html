Selected work

Four systems, each built around evidence that has to survive contact with a real decision.

Product investment01

A recurring view of where product investment returns value.

I own analysis for Audible’s recurring experimentation and product return-on-investment review with the Chief Product & Analytics Officer. The work makes tradeoffs visible without exposing confidential dollar claims.

Delivered
A durable decision review, not a one-off report.
Experimentation02

Synthetic experiments checked against experiments that already ran.

I built an agent-based system that simulates how different users respond to product changes. Its output is graded against prior web experiments, so usefulness is measured against observed behavior rather than persuasive model prose.

Method
Generate responses, aggregate effects, compare with historical reads, inspect errors.

Scientific search04

Search broadly, then let physics decide what survives.

I built a computational-physics research prototype for topological-material and dark-matter parameter search. Candidate ideas move through numerical sweeps and deterministic checks instead of asking a language model to judge its own output.

Boundary
Models propose. Numerical kernels evaluate.
